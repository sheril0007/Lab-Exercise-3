## AIM:
To design and compare the mobile UI layouts of Etsy and Depop by creating user-friendly screens with consistent navigation, visual hierarchy, and interactive elements using Figma.

## ALGORITHM:
1. Select Etsy and Depop as competing websites.
2. Identify the main user task: searching and purchasing a product.
3. Observe the homepage and navigation of both websites.
4. Evaluate the search and product-discovery process.
5. Identify usability issues using heuristic principles.
6. Compare the strengths and weaknesses of each website.
7. Select the major UI/UX problems that affect users.
8. Redesign the problematic screens with improved layouts.
9. Compare the original and redesigned versions using A/B testing.
10. Analyze user responses and finalize the improved design.

## Comparison – Etsy

Etsy provides a strong product-discovery experience through categories, search, personalized collections, and filters. Its focus on handmade, personalized, and vintage products gives the interface a unique shopping experience.

Issues identified:

Homepage can contain many promotional sections.
Large number of product choices can make selection difficult.
Product information can appear visually dense.
Important filters can be made more noticeable.

Proposed changes:

Improve visual hierarchy.
Reduce unnecessary promotional content.
Provide clearer product categories.
Make filters and sorting options easier to find.

## Comparison - Depop
Depop focuses on discovering and buying second-hand fashion, with features such as shopping by style, popular searches, and price-based browsing.

Issues identified:

Fashion-focused content can make the interface visually busy.
Product discovery may require multiple interactions.
Filter and sorting options can be made more prominent.
Product details can be organized more clearly.

Proposed changes:

Improve product categorization.
Make filters easier to access.
Increase spacing between product information.
Use clearer visual hierarchy for price, product name, and seller details.

## UI/UX Principles Used:

Consistency – Maintain consistent buttons, fonts, icons, and layouts.                                               
Visual Hierarchy – Highlight important information such as product name, price, and CTA.                                              
Proximity – Keep related product information grouped together.                                                   
Similarity – Use similar styling for similar products and controls.                                         
Contrast – Make important actions visually prominent.                                                        
Whitespace – Provide sufficient spacing to reduce visual clutter.                                                         
Visibility – Make search, filters, and navigation easy to locate.                                                                        
Feedback – Give users confirmation after actions such as adding an item.                                                  
Recognition over Recall – Use familiar icons and clearly labelled controls.                                              
Error Prevention – Prevent incorrect actions and provide clear validation.                                   

## Mobile UI Designs

Etsy: Designed a clean shopping interface with category icons, search bar, product cards, recommendations, and simple bottom navigation.

Depop: Designed a fashion-focused interface with search, style categories, product grid, recommendations, and easy navigation.

## Interaction Behaviours
Tap: Open products, categories, and buttons.                                           
Swipe: Browse product images and listings.                                            
Scroll: View more products and recommendations.                                      
Search: Find products using keywords.                                             
Filter/Sort: Narrow down product results.                                           
Add to Cart/Favourite: Save or purchase selected products.                                                
Feedback: Display confirmation after user actions.                                      

## Comparison of UI Designs

Etsy: More focused on handmade and personalized products, with category-based discovery and recommendations.

Depop: More focused on fashion discovery, using visual product cards and style-based browsing.

Overall: The improved designs provide better spacing, clearer hierarchy, easier navigation, and more visible actions.
